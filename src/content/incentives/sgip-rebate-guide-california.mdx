If you live in California, the state has allocated funds to assist homeowners installing batteries through the **Self-Generation Incentive Program (SGIP)**.

For eligible homeowners, SGIP can significantly reduce the cost of an energy storage system.

**August 2026 status:** Availability varies by SGIP budget category and programme administrator. Some budgets are exhausted or waitlisted. Being placed on a waitlist does not guarantee funding and depends on future CPUC appropriations. Check current [SGIP Program Metrics](https://www.selfgenca.com/home/program_metrics) and waitlists before relying on an incentive. 

---

## Part 1: The Three "Buckets" of Money

Your address, income, and medical needs determine which SGIP budget you may access.

### Bucket A: Residential Solar and Storage Equity
*   **Rebate Amount**: Up to **$1,100 per kWh**.
*   **Who Qualifies?**: Subject to specific income thresholds and eligibility requirements.
*   **Status**: This is the current active Equity budget. Funding is subject to availability and potential waitlists.

### Bucket B: Equity Resiliency and Small Residential Storage
*   **Status**: The CPUC identified these funds as available only through 2025. They are not an active funding stream for new 2026 applications unless you are on an existing waitlist that receives new appropriations. 
*   **Historical Note**: Equity Resiliency historically required living in a Tier 2/Tier 3 High Fire Threat District (HFTD) AND experiencing two or more PSPS events OR having a Medical Baseline allowance.

### Bucket C: General Market
*   **Rebate Amount**: **$150 per kWh**.
*   **Who Qualifies?**: Any PG&E, SCE, SDG&E, or SoCalGas customer who doesn't fit into the equity buckets.
*   **The Catch**: Funding for this bucket is limited. In some territories, the General Market funds are exhausted, creating a waitlist.

## Part 2: Medical Baseline Eligibility

One way to qualify for certain SGIP tiers is through the **Medical Baseline Allowance**.
Do you or anyone in your home use:
*   An **electric wheelchair** or scooter?
*   A **CPAP machine** for sleep apnea?
*   A **nebulizer** for asthma?
*   A **motorized bed**?
*   An electric **well pump** (and it's your primary source of water)?

If yes, you may qualify.
1.  **Apply**: Download the Medical Baseline application form from your utility's website.
2.  **Sign**: Have your doctor sign it.
3.  **Submit**: Submit it to the utility.
4.  **Wait**: Once the "Medical Baseline" line item appears on your monthly bill, you can use it as proof of eligibility for applicable SGIP equity programs.

## Part 3: The Application Process

SGIP is a multi-step process that can take several months.

### Phase 1: Reservation Request
Your installer (the "Developer") submits the application.
*   **Documents**: Contract, Site Plan, Customer Authorization.
*   **Result**: You receive a "Confirmed Reservation Letter." If you are relying on the incentive, do not install before receiving confirmation.

### Phase 2: Proof of Project Milestone (PPM)
*   You must prove the project is moving forward.
*   **Documents**: Signed building permit or equipment purchase order.

### Phase 3: Incentive Claim Form (ICF)
*   The system is installed, inspected, and interconnected.
*   **Documents**: Final Building Permit, Photo of the Battery Serial Number, Monitoring Confirmation.

### Phase 4: Payment Review
*   The Program Administrator (PA) reviews the final claim.
*   **Result**: If approved, payment is issued. Payment timelines vary significantly based on administrator volume.

## Part 4: Tax Implications (The Fine Print)

Is the rebate taxable income? **It depends.**

1.  **Section 136 Exclusion**: Generally, subsidies provided by public utilities for energy conservation measures are *not* included in gross income.
2.  **Basis Reduction**: However, if you claim the rebate tax-free, you must reduce the "basis" of your property for the Federal ITC.
    *   **Fed ITC (Pre-2026 installation only)**: For an eligible pre-2026 installation, you could only claim the 30% credit on the net cost after deducting the SGIP rebate. This credit is not available for new 2026 purchases.
3.  **Alternative**: If you report the rebate as income (taxable), you could claim the ITC on the full gross cost.
    *   Consult a CPA. This guidance applies to historical or carry-forward claims, not new 2026 purchases.

## Part 5: Top Rejection Reasons

Why do applications fail?

1.  **Wrong Address on Medical Baseline**: The name on the utility bill must match the name on the SGIP application. If the bill is in your husband's name and the SGIP app is in your name, it gets flagged.
2.  **Equipment Not Verified**: The battery must be on the **SGIP Verified Equipment List**. (All Tesla, Enphase, FranklinWH units are). If you install a sketchy DIY battery from AliExpress, you get $0.
3.  **Warranty Failure**: The battery must have a 10-year warranty.
4.  **No Monitoring**: The utility requires visibility into the battery's performance. If you disconnect the WiFi, they can claw back the rebate.

## Glossary of Terms

*   **Program Administrator (PA)**: The entity running SGIP in your area (PG&E, SCE, CSE).
*   **Host Customer**: You (the homeowner).
*   **Developer**: The installer submitting the paperwork.
*   **PPM (Proof of Project Milestone)**: The deadline to show you have a permit.
*   **ICF (Incentive Claim Form)**: The final paperwork to get paid.

## The History of SGIP

SGIP started in 2001, long before lithium batteries. It was originally designed to encourage businesses to install their own generation during the energy crisis.
In 2018, the state pivoted SGIP toward **Energy Storage** for vulnerable communities facing "Public Safety Power Shutoffs" (PSPS). 
The program continues to evolve, with new budget categories prioritizing low-income residents and equity communities.

## Engineering Reality

The SGIP programme's structure is well-documented, but several operational features create meaningful gaps between the published rebate amount and the actual value received by eligible homeowners.

**SGIP rebates are paid per kWh of installed capacity, not per kWh of energy delivered.** An SGIP rebate of $150/kWh (General Market) applied to a 13.5 kWh Tesla Powerwall yields: 13.5 × $150 = $2,025 in total rebate. This rebate is calculated on the rated nameplate capacity, not on usable capacity (which is typically 90% of nameplate for most products). The homeowner receives the rebate quantum based on nameplate, regardless of whether the system actually delivers the full nameplate capacity over its lifetime.

**Incentive budget exhaustion at step levels creates funding cliff risks.** SGIP funding is structured in tranches at different step incentive levels. As each step's funding is exhausted, the programme moves to a new step with a lower incentive rate. The CPUC does not announce step transitions in advance — they are triggered automatically when the prior step's budget is committed. Confirming the current step incentive rate on the day of application — not the day of the quote — is the operationally correct approach.

**The 10-year operational commitment requirement is often overlooked in summary guides.** SGIP applications require the homeowner to commit to maintaining the installed battery system in operation for 10 years. If the system is decommissioned, sold, or removed within this period, a portion of the rebate may be recaptured. For a homeowner who plans to sell a property within 5 years, the recapture risk should be assessed before accepting the SGIP rebate on this basis. In practice, property transfers with an installed battery system typically allow the new owner to assume the remaining SGIP obligation, but this must be formally documented in the property transfer agreement.

## When This Approach Breaks Down

SGIP requires explicit attention to programme rules in several circumstances.

**Customers served by publicly owned utilities.** SGIP is funded through a surcharge on IOU (PG&E, SCE, SDG&E, SoCalGas) customer bills and is administered by the CPUC. Customers of Sacramento Municipal Utility District (SMUD), LADWP, and other POUs do not pay the SGIP surcharge and are not eligible for SGIP rebates. POU customers may have access to their utility's own battery rebate programmes, which vary significantly. 

**Aggregated or multi-unit residential properties.** SGIP applications for individual units within a multi-unit residential building (apartment, condominium) must navigate the utility's interconnection rules for shared meters and submetering. Where the building has a master meter (common in older multi-unit buildings), individual units may not be eligible for individual customer rebates under the SGIP residential programme.

**Systems installed by non-SGIP-registered contractors.** SGIP applications must be submitted by an SGIP-registered contractor. Not all MCS-certified or CSLB-licensed contractors are SGIP-registered. An installation by a non-SGIP-registered contractor, even if the work is technically compliant, cannot generate an SGIP rebate application. Confirming that the installer holds current SGIP registration (verifiable via the CPUC's SGIP contractor database) before signing a contract is a prerequisite for rebate eligibility.

## Engineering Recommendation

SGIP provides meaningful financial support for eligible California residents. Given the expiration of the federal residential ITC, SGIP is a primary factor in California battery economics.

**Priority actions for California homeowners who may be eligible:**
1. Confirm income-eligibility for the Residential Solar and Storage Equity budget, or medical baseline status if applicable.
2. Confirm the current SGIP step rate on the CPUC's SGIP portal on the day of application submission — not the day of your quote.
3. Confirm the installer's SGIP registration number before signing any contract.

**Stacking SGIP with other incentives:**
- For eligible 2025 filers with carry-forward credits, SGIP reduces the federal ITC basis. Your tax professional should calculate the adjusted ITC basis.
- SGIP does not affect California's existing property tax exemption on solar and battery installations.
- SGIP may be combinable with some utility programmes — confirm with your IOU's energy efficiency programmes team.
